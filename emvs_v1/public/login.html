Member Login

Sign in with the email address and password that you used during registration. Only logged-in members can vote.

Welcome Back

Your account keeps your vote safe and private. After you sign in, the system checks that your name is on the voter list before it shows the ballot.

  • Use the same email that you wrote on the register page.
  • Passwords are case sensitive, so check your keyboard.
  • One account can vote only one time in one election.
  • If you forget your password, contact the election office.

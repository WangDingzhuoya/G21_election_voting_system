Why Register?

Registration connects your real student identity with your online voting account. This step keeps the election fair, because every student gets exactly one vote.

  • Registration is free for all active students.
  • You only need to fill this form once.
  • Your password is never shown to other people.
  • After registration you can log in and vote.

Register Form

Fields marked with * are required.

Step 1 of 1 Complete all fields, then press the Register button.

Use your school email so the office can contact you.

Password Rules
  • Use at least 8 characters.
  • Include at least one number.
  • Include at least one big letter.

Both password fields must be the same.

How It Works

Four steps take you from a new account to a counted vote.

1
Register

Fill in your student details and create a free account.

2
Log In

Use your email and password to enter the voting area.

3
Choose

Read each candidate profile and pick the one you support.

4
Submit

Send your ballot once, and keep the receipt code safe.

Voting Rules

Each registered voter may send only one ballot in one election. Your choice stays secret, and the final count is published after the election closes. If you have a problem with your account, please contact the election office before the deadline.
